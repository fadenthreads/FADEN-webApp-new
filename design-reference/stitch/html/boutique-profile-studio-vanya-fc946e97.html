The Atelier Story

Studio Vanya is a celebration of artisanal craftsmanship and modern silhouettes. For over 12 years, we have been crafting bespoke luxury wear that honors the rich heritage of Indian textiles while pushing the boundaries of contemporary design.

Our philosophy centers on 'Experimental Luxury' — pieces that are intellectually curious, beautifully constructed, and deeply personal. Every garment is a dialogue between the artisan and the wearer.
